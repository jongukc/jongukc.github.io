\section{About Me}

I am a Ph.D. student in the \href{https://compsec.snu.ac.kr}{CompSec Lab} at
Seoul National University, advised by Prof.\
\href{https://lifeasageek.github.io}{Byoungyoung Lee}. My research interests
lie in systems security, with a focus on LLM serving systems and confidential
computing. Most recently I built \textbf{KVGuard}, a sanitizer that finds
memory-safety bugs in the KV cache of LLM serving engines such as vLLM and
SGLang.
